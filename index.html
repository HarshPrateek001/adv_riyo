Your Questions, Answered with Care

Here are some common questions we receive. If you don't find your answer here, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.

How much does an obituary ad cost?

The cost depends on the newspaper, city, ad size, and whether it's black & white or color. Our booking widget provides an instant estimate once you make your selections.

Can I see a preview of my ad before it's published?

Absolutely. We provide a free digital mockup (proof) of your ad for your approval before it is sent to the newspaper for publication.

What is the deadline for booking an ad for tomorrow's newspaper?

Deadlines vary by newspaper, but generally, for next-day publication, bookings should be completed before 4 PM on the preceding day. We recommend booking at least 2 days in advance to be safe.

What if my city or newspaper is not listed?

Please contact our 24x7 helpline. We have connections with over 200+ newspapers and can likely arrange your ad booking even if it's not listed on the website.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, and various mobile wallets for secure online payment.
